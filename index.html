Understanding Typography in Document Design

Choosing the right font affects how your reader perceives your document:

  • Helvetica (Sans-Serif): Clean, modern, and highly readable. Excellent for reports, official letters, and tech-focused documents.
  • Times New Roman (Serif): Traditional and authoritative. The standard choice for legal forms, academic papers, and highly formal correspondence.
  • Courier (Monospace): Technical and structured. Ideal for computer code, raw data sheets, draft manuscripts, and typewriter-style designs.

Why Use PDF Format for Your Documents?

The Portable Document Format (PDF) is the universal standard for sharing professional files. Unlike text files or doc drafts, a PDF guarantees that your document layout, margins, and formatting look exactly the same on any screen, smartphone, or printer. It is the preferred file format for job applications, resume letters, contracts, academic papers, and official reports.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure on PDF Assistant?

Yes, absolutely. PDF Assistant uses client-side rendering technology. None of the text, formatting styles, or loaded templates are transmitted to our servers or saved in a remote database. The entire PDF compilation is performed locally on your device, ensuring total privacy.

Can I edit page margins and size?

Yes. You can toggle between standard A4 paper size (standard in Europe and internationally) and US Letter paper size (standard in North America). The builder automatically aligns your margins and handles line wrapping correctly for both.

Yes. Our text-to-pdf utility contains paragraph settings for Left, Center, Right, and Justified text alignment. You can also adjust line height spacing (Single spacing to Double spacing) to ensure your document conforms to formal layout guidelines.
