Free Online PDF to PDF/A Converter

PDF Assistant provides a browser-native archiver utility designed to compile and transform standard PDFs into ISO-compliant PDF/A files suitable for long-term document preservation. PDF/A ensures your documents (such as legal agreements, invoices, statements, or books) will render precisely the same way across all future hardware and software configurations.

How to Convert PDF to PDF/A Online

Creating a long-term PDF/A archive copy is quick and simple:

  1. Select your file: Drag and drop your standard PDF document into the upload card or click to browse files locally.
  2. Configure Profile: Choose between standard PDF/A-2b (which allows JPEG/lossless image compression and modern structures) or PDF/A-1b (basic conformance).
  3. Compile and Save: Click "Convert to PDF/A". The local engine modifies the catalog dictionary structure and applies the conformance flags. Review the document preview and download your archived PDF/A.

Why is PDF/A Required for Long-Term Archiving?

Standard PDFs are highly flexible but can contain dynamic features that prevent accurate rendering decades from now:

  • Device-Independent Colors: PDF/A requires specified color profiles (such as sRGB OutputIntents) so that color reproduction remains consistent across future screens.
  • Metadata Standards: Explicit XML metadata schemas (XMP) are embedded directly inside the document catalog to facilitate machine indexing.
  • No External References: Linking to external fonts or web resources is prohibited; everything must be self-contained.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between standard PDF and PDF/A?

A standard PDF can link to external websites, use system fonts, contain audio/video media, and use JavaScript. PDF/A strictly forbids these features to ensure the file remains completely self-contained and universally readable for decades.

What conformance profiles do you support?

We support **PDF/A-2b** (Level B basic visual conformance for modern workflows) and **PDF/A-1b** (Level B basic conformance for older document structures).

Are my archived documents uploaded online?

No. All catalog updates and metadata conversions are executed locally inside your web browser. Your private tax logs or corporate templates are never transmitted to external cloud systems.

How do I verify the conformed PDF/A file?

You can open the downloaded document in standard checkers (such as Adobe Acrobat Reader Pro's Preflight tool or VeraPDF) to confirm compliance with ISO-19005 standards.
